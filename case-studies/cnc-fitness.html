CNC Fitness

CNC Fitness is a modern fitness mobile app designed to help users track workouts, follow healthy habits, and stay motivated. The app offers workout plans, nutrition guidance, and progress tracking through a simple, user-friendly experience.

RoleUI/UX App Designer
Timeline4 weeks
PlatformMobile app
CategoryFitness / Health & Wellness

The challenge

Fitness apps can overwhelm users with too much information, making it difficult to track progress and stay consistent and motivated. XFit Fitness series target individuals seeking structured motivation and measurable progress in their fitness routines — the response needed a clear, motivating experience that makes workouts and progress easy to understand, not another data-dense dashboard.

Brand & logo

The identity pairs a bold "X" mark with the CNC Fitness wordmark on a stark black-and-lime palette — chosen to feel energetic and gym-native rather than soft or wellness-generic, carried from the app icon through to supporting lifestyle photography.

UX structure

A structured user flow connects goals, workouts, nutrition and results without friction, built on a documented design system (100+ variables/tokens) and a component library covering everything from data tables to audio-style progress bars — wireframed in low-fidelity before any visual polish was applied.

Core screens

A simple onboarding flow (weight, gender, height) builds a personalized fitness plan around the user's goals and abilities; from there, the home, workout and nutrition screens act as a daily companion — combining AI-powered recommendations, motivational feedback and easy access to progress.

Outcome

A complete fitness app concept — from a documented problem and target audience, through branding, a structured design system, and a full onboarding-to-coaching screen set — built around the idea that a fitness app's job is to reduce friction and overwhelm, not add to it.

4weeks, discovery to final UI
100+design tokens/variables in the documented system
1consistent black-and-lime identity, app icon to coaching screen

The discipline here was editorial restraint on a data-heavy product — choosing what the home screen shows by default versus what stays one tap away, so the app motivates instead of overwhelming.
